Who we are

A brighter beginning can change a lifetime.

S.A.B.R.E.E. Enrichment Academy, Inc. is committed to nurturing children and strengthening the adults and communities who support them.

Our approach brings together education, advocacy, social-emotional development, family engagement, and forward-looking learning experiences. We believe children thrive when their safety, dignity, curiosity, and potential are protected.

From the Womb to the Classroom (and Beyond)

A child-centered promise rooted in dignity, healing, learning, and opportunity.

S.A.B.R.E.E. Enrichment Academy transforms tragedy into opportunity by empowering children, youth, and families through trauma-informed education, mentorship, and healing-centered enrichment. Founded in honor of Sabree Marquis Daniel and Renay Daniel, the organization is committed to breaking cycles of trauma and inequity while inspiring the next generation to thrive, lead, and create lasting, positive change.

A world where every life is cherished and communities flourish—where individuals are empowered to heal, grow, and succeed. S.A.B.R.E.E. advances dignity, nurturing, early intervention, equitable education, holistic care, and healing-centered leadership to help break generational cycles of trauma.

Named in honor of Sabree Marquis Daniel and Renay Daniel, S.A.B.R.E.E. grew from resilience and a commitment to transform grief into purpose. The organization works to empower youth and families, especially those from historically marginalized communities. Dr. Sonji Fatima Daniel Harold serves as CEO and Founder.

  • Equity and Access: Advance fair access to healthcare, education, and enrichment.
  • Trauma-Informed Advocacy: Center safety, understanding, and healing.
  • Systemic Reform: Challenge barriers that affect child and family well-being.
  • Empowerment and Resilience: Support leadership, mentorship, and community strength.

  • Advance equitable access to healthcare and education.
  • Promote healing-centered, trauma-informed advocacy.
  • Dismantle systemic barriers to well-being.
  • Empower marginalized communities through leadership and mentorship.

  • Advance holistic and equitable early childhood education for ages 0–8, including neurodivergent children.
  • Address health disparities that impact educational outcomes through family-centered support.
  • Honor Sabree Marquis Daniel’s legacy through healing-centered practice.
  • Strengthen families and communities with caregiver resources.
  • Support sustainability through grants, partnerships, and diversified funding.
  • Measure impact and drive continuous innovation using evidence-informed tools.

Strategic direction

Turning purpose into practical progress.

Our strategic priorities focus on building capacity, deepening community connections, and making child-centered learning and advocacy more accessible.

Strengthen family and caregiver knowledge

Offer clear, supportive educational resources for mothers, fathers, and caregivers.

Build emotionally safe learning environments

Share approaches that encourage regulation, connection, and respectful communication.

Expand access to innovation

Develop interest in STEM, augmented reality, virtual reality, AI, and immersive learning.

Connect communities to useful resources

Make information and resource pathways easier for families and professionals to find.

A Bill of Rights for Children Born Into Marginalized Communities

Every child deserves safety, dignity, opportunity, and a voice—regardless of background or circumstance.

Read the full Bill of Rights

The right to safety

To be protected from harm and supported by safe environments.

The right to dignity

To be treated with respect, compassion, and fairness.

The right to learn

To access enriching learning and development opportunities.

The right to be heard

To have needs, experiences, and perspectives taken seriously.
